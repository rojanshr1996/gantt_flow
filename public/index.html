Gantt Flow

Transform your Google Calendar events into interactive visual Gantt chart timelines across daily, monthly, and yearly views.

Gantt Flow is an intuitive project management and schedule visualization client designed for professionals, students, and teams to prevent schedule overlaps, master time management, and export timeline reports.

Main Features

Core capabilities designed for seamless visual project and calendar management.

🗓️

Multi-Calendar Aggregation

Combine events across work, personal, and shared team Google Calendars into a unified Gantt chart view.

⏱️

Day, Month & Year Time Scales

Switch between Day (24-hour breakdown), Month (daily granularity), and Year views with smooth zoom controls.

📄

PDF Timeline Export

Export high-definition PDF Gantt chart documents for meetings, presentations, and physical printing.

📎

Google Drive File Attachments

Browse and attach relevant Google Drive document or image links directly to individual calendar events.

⚡

Bi-Directional Real-Time Sync

Any event created, modified, or deleted within Gantt Flow updates your Google Calendar instantly.

🔒

Client-Side Data Security

Your calendar and event data stay strictly between your device and Google APIs with token encryption.

Required Google Permissions & Justification

Transparent breakdown of why Gantt Flow requires specific Google Account access.

📅

Why Google Calendar is Required

Scopes: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events & https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonly

Google Calendar permission is required to:

  • Fetch your calendars and events to render the horizontal Gantt chart timeline bars.
  • Allow you to create new events directly on your Google Calendar from within the app.
  • Enable updating event start/end dates, times, titles, descriptions, and locations.
  • Allow deleting events with full real-time bi-directional synchronization.
📁

Why Google Drive is Required

Scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.readonly

Google Drive Read-Only permission is required solely to:

  • Display a list of your existing Google Drive files in a file picker when creating or editing an event.
  • Allow you to select and attach document or image reference links to specific calendar events.
  • Gantt Flow only reads file metadata to attach links; it cannot modify or delete any files in your Google Drive.
